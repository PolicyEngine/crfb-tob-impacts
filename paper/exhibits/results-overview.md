### Terminal-year static impacts

In 2100, we estimate that 100 percent taxation raises the most revenue
($1,026.6 billion) and 95 percent taxation the next most
($759.1 billion). Full repeal loses the most
($3,319.1 billion), followed by the reverse Roth
($1,045.8 billion). By then the Roth swap,
the phased Roth swap, and the short phase-in Roth reach the same end state, with
employer contributions taxable and no benefits taxed, and each loses
$547.8 billion.
